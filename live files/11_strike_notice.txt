ERASMUS AIRWAYS STRIKE NOTICE — GROUND HANDLING STAFF JULY 2026

Erasmus Airways has been notified that ground handling staff at Brussels Airport (BRU), Charleroi Airport (CRL), and Eindhoven Airport (EIN) represented by the CNE-CSC and FGTB unions have served strike notice covering the period 14–18 July 2026. Industrial action during this period may affect check-in, baggage handling, and aircraft turnaround at these airports.

Erasmus Airways is actively pursuing contingency arrangements and will provide updates as the situation develops. Passengers travelling through Brussels, Charleroi, or Eindhoven between 14 and 18 July 2026 are advised to monitor their email and the Erasmus Airways flight status page.

If Erasmus Airways cancels a flight due to strike action by its own ground handling staff, passengers are entitled to EU261 compensation as the disruption is within Erasmus Airways' sphere of control. If the strike is carried out by third-party airport staff not employed by Erasmus Airways, the disruption may qualify as extraordinary circumstances, in which case EU261 compensation would not apply, though duty of care obligations (meals, accommodation, rebooking) remain in force.

Passengers on affected routes who wish to rebook proactively to avoid disruption risk may change their flight free of charge (fare difference still applies) up to 48 hours before the original departure during the announced strike window. This waiver applies only to bookings with travel dates of 14–18 July 2026 on the three affected airports and must be processed via My Erasmus Airways.
