ERASMUS AIRWAYS SUMMER 2026 PEAK TRAVEL NOTICE

Summer 2026 is projected to be the busiest travel season in Erasmus Airways' history, with load factors across the network averaging 94% for the period 1 July – 31 August 2026. Passengers are strongly advised to check in online as early as possible and to arrive at the airport well within the recommended window.

Erasmus Airways does not hold seats or accept provisional bookings. Availability is only confirmed at the time of purchase.

Erasmus Airways will be operating additional aircraft on wet lease from partner carriers during peak summer to meet demand. Wet lease aircraft may display a different livery or aircraft type than shown at booking. Passenger rights and Erasmus Airways' standard terms and conditions apply regardless of the operating aircraft or livery.

Passengers with upcoming bookings are reminded that travel insurance is not included in any Erasmus Airways fare by default and must be purchased separately. Given the high load factors this summer, rebooking options in the event of disruption may be limited and Erasmus Airways recommends passengers consider travel insurance that covers missed connections and accommodation costs.
