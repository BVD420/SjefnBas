ERASMUS AIRWAYS AMSTERDAM SCHIPHOL OPERATIONAL NOTICE — SUMMER 2026

Amsterdam Schiphol Airport (AMS) is currently operating under a flight movement cap imposed by the Dutch government as part of ongoing noise reduction and environmental commitments. This cap is in force for the full summer 2026 season (26 March – 25 October 2026) and has resulted in reduced slot availability across all carriers operating at Schiphol, including Erasmus Airways.

As a result of the slot cap, Erasmus Airways has reduced its Schiphol-based schedule. Several routes previously operated daily have moved to fewer weekly frequencies. Passengers on cancelled frequencies have been automatically rebooked to the nearest available service and notified by email. Passengers who were rebooked without consent and prefer a full refund instead may request one via My Erasmus Airways within 30 days of receiving the rebooking notification. The standard non-refundable fare policy does not apply to schedule changes initiated by Erasmus Airways.

Schiphol is also undergoing terminal renovation works throughout summer 2026. Security queue times are currently averaging 35–45 minutes during peak hours (06:00–09:00 and 15:00–19:00). Erasmus Airways strongly recommends arriving at the airport at least 2.5 hours before departure for all Schiphol flights during this period. Missing a flight due to airport queue times does not constitute grounds for a refund or rebooking under EU261 or Erasmus Airways' standard policy.
