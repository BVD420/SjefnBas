ERASMUS AIRWAYS FUEL SURCHARGE AND PRICING UPDATE — JUNE 2026

Erasmus Airways applies a variable fuel surcharge to all tickets, reviewed quarterly. Due to sustained elevated Brent crude prices above $95 per barrel through Q2 2026, the current fuel surcharge has been set at €18 per flight segment for short-haul routes (under 1,500km) and €34 per flight segment for medium-haul routes (1,500–3,500km). These surcharges are included in the total fare displayed at checkout and are non-refundable on standard fares.

The surcharge is subject to revision at the next quarterly review in September 2026. Tickets purchased before a surcharge revision are not retroactively adjusted — passengers pay the surcharge in effect at the time of booking regardless of subsequent changes.

Erasmus Airways does not offer fuel surcharge waivers for any fare class, including Flexi Plus. The surcharge applies to all passengers including infants, though infant surcharges are capped at 50% of the adult rate.

Passengers who booked before March 2026 and have seen a surcharge added to a previously confirmed booking may request a full refund of the surcharge difference via the Customer Verification Form. This applies only to bookings where the surcharge was added after ticket purchase, not to new bookings made under the current surcharge structure.
