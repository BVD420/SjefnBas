ERASMUS AIRWAYS FIFA WORLD CUP 2026 TRAVEL ADVISORY

The FIFA World Cup 2026 takes place across the United States, Canada, and Mexico from 11 June to 19 July 2026. Erasmus Airways does not operate its own long-haul flights. Passengers travelling to World Cup destinations will require a connecting transatlantic service operated by a partner carrier.

Erasmus Airways is not responsible for missed transatlantic connections caused by delays on its short-haul feeder services, unless the Erasmus Airways segment and the transatlantic segment are booked as a single through-ticket. Passengers with separately booked tickets must pursue compensation from each carrier independently.

Demand for flights to major European hub airports — including Amsterdam Schiphol (AMS), London Heathrow (LHR), and Paris Charles de Gaulle (CDG) — is significantly elevated throughout June and July 2026 due to World Cup travel. Erasmus Airways strongly recommends allowing a minimum of 3 hours connection time at hub airports during this period and booking as early as possible.
