ERASMUS AIRWAYS MIDDLE EAST ROUTE ADVISORY — JUNE 2026

Erasmus Airways operates routes to Tel Aviv (TLV), Amman (AMM), and Beirut (BEY). Due to ongoing regional instability and airspace restrictions across parts of the Eastern Mediterranean, all three routes are currently subject to operational advisories.

Tel Aviv routes are operating with modified flight paths avoiding Lebanese and Syrian airspace. This has added approximately 35–50 minutes to flight times on Amsterdam–Tel Aviv services. Passengers on affected routes will not receive EU261 delay compensation for delays solely attributable to the mandated airspace rerouting, as this falls under extraordinary circumstances outside Erasmus Airways' control.

Beirut services remain suspended as of 1 April 2026 following guidance from the Dutch government (Ministerie van Buitenlandse Zaken) advising against all but essential travel to Lebanon. Passengers holding Erasmus Airways tickets to Beirut on any date from 1 April 2026 onwards are entitled to a full refund or free rebooking to an alternative destination of equal or lesser fare value. To claim, visit My Erasmus Airways or contact the disruption claims team.

Amman services are operating normally. Erasmus Airways continues to monitor the situation and reserves the right to suspend or reroute services with as little as 72 hours notice in the event of a material change in the security situation. Passengers travelling to Amman are advised to check the Dutch government travel advisory at nederlandwereldwijd.nl before departure.

Erasmus Airways will not be liable for hotel, transport, or consequential costs incurred by passengers who choose not to travel on routes that have not been formally suspended, where the Dutch government travel advisory does not recommend against travel.
