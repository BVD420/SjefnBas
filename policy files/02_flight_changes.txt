ERASMUS AIRWAYS FLIGHT CHANGES AND CANCELLATIONS

Minor name corrections (for example, fixing a typo of up to 3 characters) are free and can be done via My Erasmus Airways up to 2 hours before departure. Full name changes are treated as a new booking: the original ticket is non-refundable and a new ticket must be purchased at the current fare.

Passengers may change their flight date, time, or route via My Erasmus Airways or the app. A flight change fee applies (€45 per person per flight for regular fares), plus any fare difference if the new flight is more expensive. Changes must be made at least 2.5 hours before the original departure time.

Erasmus Airways tickets are non-refundable by default. If you cancel a booking, you will not receive a refund for the base fare. Airport taxes are refundable (minus a €20 administration fee) if requested within 1 month of the flight date.

Passengers who purchased a Flexi Plus fare may change their flight once at no charge (fare difference still applies) and receive a full refund if they cancel at least 24 hours before departure. Flexi Plus upgrades can be purchased after booking via My Erasmus Airways.

Erasmus Airways sells travel insurance at booking through a third-party provider. Cancellation coverage under this insurance is subject to the insurer's terms, not Erasmus Airways', and must be claimed directly with the insurer. Erasmus Airways cannot process insurance claims.
