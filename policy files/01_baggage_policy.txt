ERASMUS AIRWAYS BAGGAGE POLICY

Every Erasmus Airways passenger is entitled to one small personal bag (40cm x 20cm x 25cm) free of charge. This must fit under the seat in front of you. Bags larger than this will not be accepted at the gate without a paid baggage add-on.

Passengers who purchase Priority Boarding may bring one additional cabin bag (55cm x 40cm x 20cm, max 10kg) into the overhead locker. Without Priority, this second bag must be checked at the gate for a fee of €25.

Checked baggage must be pre-booked online. Options are 20kg (€8–40 depending on route and timing), 25kg (€12–60), and 32kg (€24–80). Bags booked at the airport cost significantly more. A maximum of one checked bag per passenger per booking is permitted unless additional bags are purchased separately.

Bags over the pre-purchased weight limit are charged at €11 per kg at the airport. Bags exceeding 32kg are not accepted under any circumstances and must be split across multiple bags or shipped separately.

Sports equipment such as bicycles, golf bags, skis, and surfboards must be declared at booking and are subject to specific fees (€60–100 depending on item and route). Sports equipment is transported in addition to, not instead of, a checked baggage allowance.
