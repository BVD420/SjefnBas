ERASMUS AIRWAYS SPECIAL ASSISTANCE

Passengers requiring wheelchair assistance or other mobility support must notify Erasmus Airways at least 48 hours before departure via the Special Assistance team, accessible through the Help section of the website. Failure to pre-notify may result in delays at the airport, though Erasmus Airways is legally obligated to provide assistance regardless.

Passengers with reduced mobility are entitled to complimentary wheelchair assistance at all airports. This service is coordinated between Erasmus Airways and the airport operator — passengers should confirm directly with both the departure and arrival airport that the request has been received.

Passengers requiring medical equipment such as oxygen concentrators or insulin must declare these at booking. Erasmus Airways does not supply medical oxygen on board. Approved portable oxygen concentrators may be carried subject to prior approval and a medical certificate.

Infants under 2 years old may travel on a parent's lap for a small fee of €25 per flight. One infant per adult is permitted. Infants are not allocated a separate seat unless a car seat is purchased. Car seats must be FAA-approved and declared at booking.
