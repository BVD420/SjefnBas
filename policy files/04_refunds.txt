ERASMUS AIRWAYS REFUNDS POLICY

Standard fares are non-refundable. Once purchased, the base fare cannot be refunded under any circumstances except where required by law or where Erasmus Airways cancels the flight.

If you choose not to travel and cancel your booking, you may claim a refund of government taxes and airport charges but not the base fare. Submit the request via My Erasmus Airways within 1 month of the flight date. A €20 administration fee is deducted from the refund.

If Erasmus Airways cancels your flight, you are entitled to a full refund of the total amount paid, including all fees and add-ons. Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days to the original payment method. Alternatively, you may choose a free rebooking to the next available flight on the same route.

If you believe a refund has been incorrectly refused, you may raise a complaint via the Erasmus Airways Customer Verification Form on the website. Erasmus Airways will review the complaint within 28 days. Further escalation options include your national ADR scheme or small claims court.

Fares purchased as part of a promotional campaign such as seat sales or Black Friday offers are subject to the same non-refundable terms as standard fares. The promotional nature of the fare does not confer additional cancellation rights.
