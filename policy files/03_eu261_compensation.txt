ERASMUS AIRWAYS EU261 FLIGHT DISRUPTION COMPENSATION

Regulation EU261/2004 entitles passengers on flights departing from an EU airport, or arriving at an EU airport on an EU-based carrier, to compensation in the event of significant delay, cancellation, or denied boarding — unless the disruption was caused by extraordinary circumstances outside the airline's control.

For delays of 3 hours or more at the final destination, passengers are entitled to compensation as follows: €250 for flights under 1,500km; €400 for flights between 1,500km and 3,500km; €400 for all flights within the EU over 1,500km. These amounts apply to delays measured at arrival, not departure.

If Erasmus Airways cancels a flight and notifies the passenger fewer than 14 days before departure, the same compensation amounts apply as for delays. If notified 14 days or more in advance, no compensation is due, though a full refund or re-routing must be offered.

Compensation is not payable when the disruption is caused by circumstances beyond Erasmus Airways' control, including severe weather, air traffic control strikes, security incidents, or political instability. Erasmus Airways must demonstrate the extraordinary circumstance — it cannot simply assert it.

EU261 claims must be submitted via the Erasmus Airways website under Help > Flight Disruption Claims. Erasmus Airways has 14 days to respond. If the claim is rejected or ignored, passengers may escalate to their national aviation authority (for example, the CAA in the UK or the ILT in the Netherlands) or a certified ADR scheme. Claims may be submitted up to 6 years after the flight in England and Wales, 5 years in Scotland, or 2 years in most EU jurisdictions.

Regardless of whether compensation is owed, Erasmus Airways must provide meals and refreshments for delays of 2 hours or more, hotel accommodation for overnight delays, and transport between the hotel and the airport. Passengers may claim reasonable expenses if Erasmus Airways fails to provide these by submitting receipts via the disruption claims portal.
