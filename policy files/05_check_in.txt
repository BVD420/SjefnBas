ERASMUS AIRWAYS CHECK-IN AND BOARDING

Online check-in opens 60 days before departure and closes 2 hours before departure. Passengers who do not check in online will be charged an airport check-in fee of €55 per person per flight. This fee is not waivable except in cases of Erasmus Airways system failure, which must be reported at the airport.

Once checked in, boarding passes must be downloaded and either printed or saved to a mobile device. Erasmus Airways does not accept screenshots — the barcode must be scannable. A boarding pass reprint at the airport costs €20.

Boarding begins 30 minutes before departure and gates close 20 minutes before departure. Passengers who arrive at the gate after closure will not be permitted to board and are not entitled to a refund or rebooking at no charge.

Seats can be selected at booking or added later via My Erasmus Airways. Passengers who do not select a seat will be assigned one randomly at check-in at no charge, though Erasmus Airways cannot guarantee seat allocation preferences such as window seats, exit rows, or seats together for families on unassigned passengers.
